00 Where We're Headed
The thesis
AR is becoming a Relationship Operating System
AR is shifting from a marketing automation tool to a suite of agents that helps agencies keep clients longer and convert prospects faster. The moat isn't the workflow — it's being the only system that proves which actions drove retention, cross-sell, and new business. Built on 13+ AMS integrations no competitor can replicate.
Know — Decisioning
Client memory & signals
AMS data, policy signals, engagement history, carrier feeds, claims, producer activity. The system knows the book of business and what needs to happen next.
Execute — Channels
Agents that act
Email, SMS, AI voice (Vocara), websites, social, direct mail. Agents identify the opportunity, run the campaign, and hand off to the producer with context.
Prove — Attribution
Actions tied to outcomes
Which campaigns drove retention. What generated cross-sell revenue. What drove quote-to-bind. This is the layer that makes AR defensible — and turns access pricing into outcome pricing.

04 What We're Watching
Things the whole org needs to know about
TCR compliance is a live risk for ~700 Business Texting clients
Every AR Business Texting client is currently registered under a single shared TCR campaign — which is non-compliant with updated TCR requirements. If Bandwidth or TCR flags this shared campaign, every client on it loses outbound texting simultaneously with no warning. Phase 1 (Forge privacy policy auto-injection) is in progress with Devon this week. Phase 2 migrates all ~700 clients from shared to individual campaigns — Lam's team is running this manually in Bandwidth. Sales needs to stop committing texting timelines without flagging the migration.

Pressable Migration affects the whole org — August 1 is a hard deadline

The pilot (30 domains) is next — Devon is selecting them and the contract is the current gate. Support will see tickets. Enterprise clients (~53 across RAIS and KAP) cannot go through the standard migration flow and are excluded from the automated drip and lockout entirely — Enterprise Operations handles those directly.
